A panel draped on a dress form. XPBD, 40 × 40 particles.
pin / drag PINCH a point of fabric and move it — mouse: left-drag.
cut TRACE a line across the fabric — mouse: shift + drag. Cuts are grid-aligned: a cut deletes constraints, so the solver never sees a topology change.
gather FIST near the fabric bunches it — mouse: right-click near it.
smooth PALM_PUSH presses a drape flat — mouse: wheel over it.
bias SPREAD both hands stretches on the bias.
orbit mouse: right-drag away from the fabric.
